## The film

- Scott (15 Nov, after the lit windows): "now do an original idea" (for TikTok). A hotel lift in black lacquer and
  brass, the CB inlaid across its two doors so it splits down the middle each time they open; "Going up?" over the doors
  from frame one (the hook and cover). A brass dial above counts G, 1, 2, 3. A soft bell, the doors part and the camera
  goes in through them onto each floor, real footage, the floor in the house label's order (gold caps, underline, cream
  Cormorant): GROUND FLOOR / Arrivals (Rowton at dusk), FIRST FLOOR / The spa (Rowton's pool), SECOND FLOOR / Dinner
  (Fairlawns' food spread), TOP FLOOR / Do not disturb (Rutland's suite). No hotel names. The doors close, the CB whole
  again; it turns gold as the lift falls dark and becomes the logo on the track's final chord, shimmer.
- Music: Jazz Cocktail Lounge (alex-morgan, Pixabay 556246), its last 21.6s from 142.64s, as lift music: every cue on
  the track's beats (`BEATS`, librosa); the doors stay shut through the band's stop (150.7 to 152s) and open as it
  comes back; the logo on the final chord (160.31s). Also on the unposted circle C (cocktail cut) and sash window.
- The bell (`public/stings/lift/ding.wav`, synthesised on the track's G, quiet under the music) is a sound effect, an
  exception to the house rule for Scott to keep or drop.

## Notes

`cbh-flipbook-winter` (`flipbook/HardbackFlip.tsx`, 27.9s, Happy Feels from 40.79s, its unused middle): a black cloth hardback with the gold CB opens, the camera pushes into the right page, each page turns to the next hotel full-bleed (Gisborough sunrise and snowy garden, Low Wood Bay sunset, Rutland bath, Rowton dusk, Waterton still; no Kingfisher), pulls back, "Which page / would you stay in?", white CB. Sent; Scott: "needs work and a polish, it messes up in the beginning and could be much more". Known faults: the cover vanishes mid-turn (around 1.8-2.0s), the book jumps sideways as the camera moves, the inside cover pops from black to cream (~3s), and each "turn" is a flat 2D squash (scaleX of cos), not a page: it needs a real curl (curved page, its back, the shadow on the page below) and more of the book seen. Charcoal background with a lighter middle (may read like the glow Scott disliked).

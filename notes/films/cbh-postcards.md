## The film

- Scott (15 Nov, after the lift: "your turn"): an original for TikTok. Postcards land one on top of another on a dark
  wooden table, photo side up, each with the house label (gold caps place, underline, cream Cormorant name). Each flips
  over on the beat: a handwritten note (Caveat) writes itself, addressed to the people back at work ("The team, back at
  the office", "Everyone still at their desks", "The team, Monday morning"), and the postmark thumps down (no sound).
  Waterton Park ("Our hotel has its own island, you cross a bridge to get in x": confirmed fact), Ruthin Castle ("Slept
  in an actual castle. There's a peacock on the wall x": the photo's peacock), Holdsworth House ("Tea by the fire every
  afternoon. Wish you were here x"). The camera goes in on the last card's stamp, a gold CB, until the stamp's CB is
  exactly the logo's CB (zoom to the logo's size and place), the table falls to black and the wordmark comes up round
  it, shimmer.
- Music: Event Music by prettyjohn1 (Pixabay 595091, first use), 152 BPM, from its drop (6.27s); a card lands every 16
  beats on the phrase starts. The postmark is visual only (no sound effects).
- Music changed (Scott, 15 Nov: "change the music"): Party Pop by atlasaudio (Pixabay 606271, first use), 143.6 BPM,
  the first card landing as the kick comes back after its first break (15.93s), a card every 16 beats. 25.1s.
- Scott (15 Nov) on Party Pop: "that's more like it, more TikToky like that". Upbeat pop over loungey for TikTok pieces.

20 Nov: the postcard labels lost their gold underline (Scott: no gold underlines), re-rendered for TikTok, 25.1s.

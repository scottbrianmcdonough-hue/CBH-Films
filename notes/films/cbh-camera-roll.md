## The film

- Scott (5 Nov, from the CBH Instagram grid on his phone: "a reel like this, like a photo album on the phone, and can do
  shifted moves"): a generic phone photo album in black and gold, a section a weekend (date, then the house label), six
  shots each: Ruthin (7 Nov), Rutland (14 Nov), Holdsworth (21 Nov, stills), Low Wood Bay (28 Nov), Gisborough (5 Dec).
  Opens on the album six across (the cover), a pinch spreads it to three across, a tap opens a weekend's first shot full
  screen, a swipe pages to the second, a swipe down sends it home, a flick to the next weekend, 8 beats a weekend; then
  "Next weekend" (one empty gold dashed slot), a pinch back, "Room for / one more?" over the dimmed wall.
- Music: Upbeat Dance Pop Infectious (toolikes, Pixabay 225269), edited by `scripts/camera-roll-prep.sh`
  (`public/campaigns/camera-roll/upbeat-pop-edit.m4a`).
- The weekend dates (7 Nov to 5 Dec) are in the past for anything posted after them: change them in the spec before
  it goes out.

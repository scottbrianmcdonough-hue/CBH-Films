## Notes

`the-mount-collage`: the moving tile wall (`collage/` engine; Scott: "dynamic squares, a moving collage", with The Mount's photos). The Mount Country Manor Hotel, Wolverhampton, and its golf club (the pavilion is signed "The Mount Golf and Country Club"): Scott's photos in `public/hotels/the-mount/`, 20 used, each entering once (pizza and adventure golf left out). On Cooking Groove (Scott's pick) to the end of the trimmed file, 23.7s (Scott: "it doesn't have to be 15 seconds, the track, don't constrain"). Tiles, not cards (Scott: "we did cards already"): a 2 x 3 grid, even gutters, small corners; the 3D is the wall's (Scott: "the 3D depth is cool if they look like tiles and not cards"): one plane tilted back and turning slowly as one, tiles with thickness (a solid edge) casting shadows, a moving tile lifting off the wall and setting flat on the hit. Shift, slide and collide on the track's accents (after "jerky", "pop and lock", "no little jerk when they stop", "more energy"): tiles slide in from the side and shove their row along (`shoved`), knocking the end tile off (`shovedOff`); a tile grows into the scene on the big hits; nothing moves once stopped; holds through the stop and the quiet bar; the scene behind shifts framing on every landing (Scott: use the background in the choreography). Label "WOLVERHAMPTON / The Mount / Country Manor / Hotel & Golf" (the full name, Scott) held ~3s (Scott: too quick), bottom row clear under it. End card over the blurred, darkened wall (Scott first wanted black, then "doesn't have to be black"): "Pudding first, / or the four-poster?" (Scott wasn't sure about "Which square / would you pick?", nor that the dessert is a fondant: never name a dish the photo may not show). Cosy autumn grade. -15.3 LUFS. Final after a polish pass (grid 10px up, clear of the CB mark). The ending (Scott: the blurred-wall end card was "kind of abrupt"): the fondant flies up out of the wall to fill the frame on the 39.5 kick (`end.zoom`), the wall dropping into shadow, holds a beat, fades to black on 40.5, the question rises on black. Two more track tries (Scott): `the-mount-collage-swing` (Bring Back the Swing, EvolvingVibes, Pixabay 617230, 120 BPM from 7.38s, 21.8s) and `the-mount-collage-shuffle` (Funk Jazz Shuffle Groove, Catch22Music, Pixabay 358859, 110 BPM, 23.7s).

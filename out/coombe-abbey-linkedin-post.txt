We're delighted to welcome Coombe Abbey Hotel in Warwickshire to Classic British Hotels.

Few hotels can tell a story like this one. An abbey since the 12th century, it has hosted Elizabeth I, played its part in the Gunpowder Plot, and today stands in 500 acres of parkland laid out by Capability Brown.

What makes it special:
🛏️ 121 bedrooms, with four poster beds, canopies and Victorian roll top baths
🌳 500 acres of gardens, parkland and lake on the doorstep
🤝 11 meeting spaces and a marquee for up to 450
🍷 Its famous Medieval Banquets when the agenda is done

[TIKTOK] A TikTok grid of its own: first three posts

Campaign: TikTok
@Bianca Thomson

Why
TikTok gets its own grid, separate from Instagram and Facebook: shorter, a text hook on the first frame in TikTok's own caption style, playful formats, no logo ending, and more posts a week. Three formats to start, each one quick to remake for another hotel or region.


1. Camera roll dump: the North West
Video: cbh-camera-roll-north-west-tiktok.mp4 (17.7s, 9:16)

Hook
A phone camera roll, every clip playing at once, under "camera roll dump: the North West". A tap opens one full screen and the roll is swiped through on the beat: the misty lake, candles and a string quartet at Low Wood Bay, then dusk, cake by the fire and the canopied bed at Rowton Hall. The last clip drops back into the roll: "0 photos of my sofa".

Files: each post has its video, a silent copy for an in-app sound (same name ending -silent.mp4) and a cover image (ending -cover.jpg).

Text style (fixed, so the grid stays consistent): white bold text on black blocks, lower case, at most two short lines, top third of the frame, always baked into the video and never added in the app. Hotel names stay in the gold and cream label.

Time slot: TikTok 8:00 PM, one a day (suggested: camera roll first, Starry Night second, gig night third)
Platforms: TikTok only

TITLE
Coombe Abbey Hotel: Where the Agenda Meets the Abbey

SUBHEAD
A 12th Century Conference Venue in 500 Acres of Warwickshire Parkland

Coombe Abbey was founded in 1150 as a Cistercian monastery and has welcomed guests as a hotel since 1995. It sits inside its own moat on the edge of Coventry, with formal gardens and an 80 acre lake laid out by Capability Brown on one side and a working events estate on the other.

With 11 meeting rooms, 121 bedrooms and a Marquee that takes up to 450 delegates theatre style, Coombe Abbey covers everything from a board meeting for eight to a company wide conference, all on one site. The hotel joins Classic British Hotels this month.

Easy to Reach, Easy to Settle
The hotel is ten minutes by car from Coventry city centre. From the M6 at junction 2, the A46 and B4027 bring delegates to the gates in a few minutes. Coventry station, with direct trains to London Euston, is a short taxi ride away, and Birmingham Airport is 14.3 miles from the hotel.
Parking is on site, with 8 electric vehicle charging points. Once through the gates, the city feels a long way off.

121 Bedrooms for Residential Events
Every bedroom at Coombe Abbey is individually designed, and many sit within the historic abbey itself.
Crown Bedchambers are the core of the house, some in the quieter Park Priory across the grounds. Feature rooms add a canopied bed or a Victorian roll top bath, and the 14 Grand Feature Bedchambers suit speakers, VIPs and award winners: The Princess Elizabeth has its own staircase up to a bathroom in the turret. Dove Cottage, a private two bedroom cottage with its own kitchen and garden, works well for a leadership team on a longer stay.
Room service runs 24 hours, with hot food until 10pm.

Meeting and Event Spaces
All 11 rooms include complimentary WiFi, unlimited tea and coffee with fresh fruit and biscuits, and an LCD projector and screen. Capacities below are the maximum for each layout.
• The Marquee, on the East Terrace, is the largest space on the estate: up to 450 theatre style or 350 on rounds, with its own bar and kitchen. Room for a full conference, an exhibition or the awards dinner that follows it.
• The High Court and Centre Court make up the Courthouse, a self contained building that was once a tennis court and its clubhouse. Each takes 120 theatre style and 180 for a reception, and the two can be hired together for exclusive use.
• The Cloisters is one of the abbey's grandest rooms, with a private function bar and space for 120 theatre style, 90 on rounds or 64 cabaret.
• The Abbeygate, the estate's mediaeval banqueting hall, seats 140 theatre style and 90 on rounds.
• The Stuart brings an Art Deco ceiling and chandelier to groups of up to 60 theatre style or 30 around a boardroom table, and suits syndicate work.
• The Walnut takes its name from floor to ceiling antique walnut panelling and seats 24 boardroom or 30 cabaret style around its ornate fireplace.
• The Kellway is laid out as a library overlooking the moat, for 18 boardroom or 30 theatre style.
• The Harrington takes 16 boardroom or 40 theatre style, and the De Camville looks out over the courtyard and the moat.
• The Griffin, panelled with a feature fireplace, is a private room for up to eight: interviews, one to ones and senior discussions.
Day Delegate packages start from £45 per person plus VAT and include a working lunch. The 24 Hour Delegate package, from £165 per person plus VAT, adds a three course dinner in one of the historic dining rooms and an overnight stay. The events team is MIA accredited, the industry's recognised quality mark for meetings and events in the UK.

Dining for Delegates
The Garden Room Restaurant is a conservatory looking onto the grounds, serving classic dishes made with locally sourced ingredients, and the hotel's afternoon tea has won awards of its own. Private dining rooms seat from 10 up to 500 guests.
For an evening that people talk about afterwards, the award winning Mediaeval Banquets turn the abbey's history into a night of feasting, song and theatre.

Team Building in 500 Acres
The estate has a designated event field, and Go Ape runs its treetop course in the woodland. Through partners Off Limits and Eventurous, the hotel can arrange activities such as Crystal Maze, and its Restart & Reconnect wellbeing package uses the parkland to help teams unwind after a heavy agenda.
Coombe Abbey's conference record speaks for itself, with M&IT Awards over the years including Gold for Best UK Unusual Venue and Gold for Best Value for Money Conference Venue.

Enquiries
For bookers looking for a venue with genuine history, flexible space and room to breathe between sessions, Coombe Abbey is well worth a visit.

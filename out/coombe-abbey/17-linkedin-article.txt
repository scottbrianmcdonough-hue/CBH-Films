TITLE
Coombe Abbey Hotel: Where the Agenda Meets the Abbey

SUBHEAD
A 12th Century Conference Venue in 500 Acres of Warwickshire Parkland

Coombe Abbey was founded in 1150 as a Cistercian monastery and has been a hotel since 1995. It sits inside its own moat on the edge of Coventry, in 500 acres of formal gardens and parkland laid out by Capability Brown, with a designated event field for groups.

With 11 meeting rooms and 121 bedrooms on one estate, Coombe Abbey runs from a board meeting for eight to a conference for 450 in the Marquee. The hotel joins Classic British Hotels this month.

Easy to Reach, Easy to Settle
The hotel is ten minutes by car from Coventry city centre. Leave the M6 at junction 2 for the A46, and the hotel is about a mile along the B4027. Coventry station, with direct trains to London Euston, is a short taxi ride away, and Birmingham Airport is 14.3 miles from the door.
Parking is on site (charged), with 8 electric vehicle charging points. Delegates arrive close to the city and spend the day surrounded by parkland.

121 Bedrooms for Residential Events
Every bedroom at Coombe Abbey is individually designed, and many are within the historic abbey itself.
Crown Bedchambers make up most of the house, some in the quieter Park Priory across the grounds. Feature rooms add a canopied bed or a Victorian roll top bath, and the 14 Grand Feature Bedchambers are the ones to save for speakers and VIPs: The Princess Elizabeth has its own staircase up to a hidden bathroom in the turret. Dove Cottage, a private two bedroom cottage with its own kitchen and garden, works well for a leadership team on a longer stay.
Room service runs 24 hours, with hot food until 10pm.

Meeting and Event Spaces
All 11 rooms include complimentary WiFi, unlimited tea and coffee with fresh fruit and biscuits, and an LCD projector and screen. Capacities below are the maximum for each layout.
• The Marquee, on the East Terrace, is the largest space on the estate: up to 450 theatre style or 350 on rounds, with its own bar and kitchen. It suits a full conference by day and the awards dinner that follows it.
• The High Court and Centre Court make up the Courthouse, a self contained building that was once a tennis court and its clubhouse. Each takes 120 theatre style and 180 for a reception, and the two can be hired together for exclusive use.
• The Cloisters is one of the abbey's grandest rooms, with a private function bar and space for 120 theatre style, 90 on rounds or 64 cabaret.
• The Abbeygate, the estate's medieval banqueting hall, seats 140 theatre style and 90 on rounds.
• The Stuart, Art Deco with an ornate ceiling and chandelier, takes 60 theatre style or 30 boardroom and works well for syndicate sessions.
• The Walnut is named for its floor to ceiling antique walnut panelling and has an ornate fireplace; it seats 24 boardroom or 30 theatre style.
• The Kellway is laid out as a library overlooking the moat, for 18 boardroom or 30 theatre style.
• The Harrington takes 16 boardroom or 40 theatre style, and the De Camville looks out over the courtyard and the moat.
• The Griffin, panelled with a feature fireplace, is a private room for up to eight: interviews, one to ones and senior discussions.
Day Delegate packages start from £45 per person plus VAT and include a working lunch. The 24 Hour Delegate package, from £165 per person plus VAT, adds a three course dinner in one of the historic dining rooms and an overnight stay. The events team is MIA accredited, the UK's recognised quality mark for meetings and events.

Dining for Delegates
The Garden Room Restaurant is a conservatory looking onto the grounds, serving classic dishes made with locally sourced ingredients, and its afternoon tea has won awards of its own. Private dining spaces take from 10 up to 500 guests.
For an evening delegates will talk about on the drive home, the award winning Medieval Banquets turn the abbey's history into a night of feasting.

Team Building in 500 Acres
The estate has a designated event field, and Go Ape runs its treetop course in the woodland. Through partners Off Limits and Eventurous, the hotel can arrange activities such as Crystal Maze, and its Restart & Reconnect wellbeing package uses the parkland to help teams unwind after a heavy agenda.
The venue's M&IT Awards include Gold for Best UK Unusual Venue and Gold for Best Value for Money Conference Venue.

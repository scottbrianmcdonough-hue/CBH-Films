Page title
Coombe Abbey Hotel: a guide to Warwickshire's 12th century abbey

Meta description (155 characters)
Stay in a 12th century abbey set in 500 acres of Capability Brown parkland near Coventry. Our guide to Coombe Abbey Hotel, its rooms, dining and grounds.

Intro
Coombe Abbey began life in 1150 as the Abbey of Cumbe. Nine centuries on, you can sleep inside its moat, with 500 acres of gardens and parkland running out to a lake, ten minutes' drive from Coventry city centre. It is the newest member of Classic British Hotels.

A history you can sleep in
For almost four hundred years this was the largest and most influential monastery in Warwickshire, until it was surrendered to Henry VIII in 1539. The Craven family bought the estate in 1622 and kept it for three centuries, adding the West Wing in 1677 and asking Capability Brown to reshape the land around the house. Coventry City Council took on the estate in 1964, and the abbey has been a hotel since 1995.

The rooms
There are 121 bedrooms, many within the historic abbey and some in Park Priory, a quieter building across the grounds.
- Grand Feature Bedchambers: 14 rooms, each designed individually, all with canopied beds and countryside views. The Princess Elizabeth has a private staircase up to a bathroom in the turret, with a roll top bath. The Mowbray is set over three levels with views of the lake and gardens. Booking a Grand Feature includes complimentary parking.
- Feature Doubles: rooms with a canopied bed or a Victorian roll top bath.
- Crown Bedchambers: individually styled rooms, many with an ornate fireplace, in the abbey or in Park Priory.
- Dove Cottage: a private cottage with two bedrooms and its own kitchen, plus a garden looking out over the parkland.

Eating and drinking
Dinner is in the Garden Room, a conservatory restaurant looking onto the grounds, and the afternoon tea there has won awards of its own. The Mediaeval Banquets turn the abbey's history into a night of feasting. For something easier there's bar food and the Café in the Park, and room service runs around the clock (hot food until 10pm).

The grounds
Capability Brown designed the grounds. From the formal gardens, the path runs down to the 80 acre lake, a Site of Special Scientific Interest and home to Warwickshire's largest heronry; bring binoculars to the bird hides and look for grey herons and kingfishers. In the woodland, Go Ape takes the brave up into the treetops.

Beyond the gates
Coventry city centre is ten minutes away by car. Your stay includes daytime entry to St Mary's Guildhall in the Cathedral Quarter, one of the city's few remaining medieval buildings, which escaped the bombing of the Second World War and is home to the Coventry Tapestry.

Getting there
By car: leave the M6 at junction 2, join the A46, then follow signs for Coombe Park; the hotel is about a mile along the B4027. Parking is chargeable (£6 up to 8 hours, £12 up to 24 hours), and there are 8 electric car charging points.
By train: Coventry station (direct to London Euston) is a short taxi ride away.
By air: Birmingham Airport is 14.3 miles away.

Good to know
Check in from 3pm, check out by 10:30am. The hotel is cashless and card only. Children are welcome, with family rooms for two adults and two children. Pets can't stay.

Founded as a monastery in 1150, Coombe Abbey now stands inside its own moat in 500 acres of Capability Brown parkland, ten minutes from Coventry city centre and a few minutes from junction 2 of the M6.

For bookers, it's one site with real range: 121 individually designed bedrooms, 11 meeting rooms from an eight seat boardroom to a Marquee for 450 theatre style, private dining for 10 to 500, and an MIA accredited events team.

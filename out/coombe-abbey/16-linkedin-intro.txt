Founded as a monastery in 1150, Coombe Abbey stands inside its own moat in 500 acres of parkland, ten minutes from Coventry city centre and just off junction 2 of the M6.

For bookers, it's one estate with real range. There are 121 individually designed bedrooms, and 11 meeting rooms run from an eight seat boardroom to a Marquee for 450 theatre style, looked after by an MIA accredited events team.

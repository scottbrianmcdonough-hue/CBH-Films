Ten minutes' drive from Coventry city centre, Coombe Abbey Hotel is a 12th century abbey set inside its own moat, in 500 acres of parkland. We're delighted to welcome the team to Classic British Hotels.

Junction 2 of the M6 is close by, Coventry station has direct trains to London Euston, and Birmingham Airport is 14.3 miles away.

💼 𝗕𝘆 𝗱𝗮𝘆: 11 meeting rooms and an MIA accredited events team. When the guest list grows, the Marquee takes up to 450 theatre style, and Go Ape is waiting in the woodland for the team challenge.

🥂 𝗕𝘆 𝗲𝘃𝗲𝗻𝗶𝗻𝗴: Dinner in the Garden Room, a glass conservatory looking onto the gardens, or a night of feasting at the award winning Mediaeval Banquets.

🌙 𝗕𝘆 𝗻𝗶𝗴𝗵𝘁: 121 individually designed bedrooms, among them 14 Grand Feature Bedchambers with canopied beds. In The Princess Elizabeth, a private staircase climbs to a bathroom hidden in the turret.

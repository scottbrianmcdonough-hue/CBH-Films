Hook
Grim November stock footage (rain in a street puddle, rain down a window, a wet dog, a bus through the rain at night, rain dripping off an umbrella) cuts on the action into our hotels doing the same thing, nicer: jam on a scone, tea poured, a spaniel walk by a lake, a steaming hot tub, a map unfolded at a bright window. "November / everywhere else" turns to "at ours" as the music's bass comes back, each hotel shot warming up out of the cold. Ends on "Which November are you having?" and the CBH logo.

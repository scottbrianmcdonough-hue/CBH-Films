Five minutes' walk from the centre of Omagh in County Tyrone, Silverbirch Hotel has been run by the same family for over 40 years. We're delighted to welcome them to Classic British Hotels.

Belfast and Derry/Londonderry are within easy reach. The Wild Atlantic Way and the Cuilcagh Boardwalk, the "stairway to heaven", are close by too.

𝗔 𝗱𝗮𝘆 𝗮𝘁 𝗦𝗶𝗹𝘃𝗲𝗿𝗯𝗶𝗿𝗰𝗵:

🤝 𝗕𝘆 𝗱𝗮𝘆: A business centre with its own reception, six meeting rooms and room to break out. When the guest list grows, Lola's takes up to 700 delegates.

🍽️ 𝗕𝘆 𝗲𝘃𝗲𝗻𝗶𝗻𝗴: Dinner at Barreta Bar & Grill, made with local produce, then a nightcap at Bertha's, an old world whiskey and gin bar.

🛏️ 𝗕𝘆 𝗻𝗶𝗴𝗵𝘁: 64 contemporary bedrooms, each with its own seating area. The extra spacious Superior and Executive rooms have super king beds.

Welcome to the collection, Silverbirch. 🙌

# Lake District itineraries (draft for fact check)

## 1 night in the Lake District

A night by Windermere: a lake cruise, Lakeland villages, and dinner with the water outside the window.

### Day 1: Windermere and Ambleside

**Morning: arrive and walk up Orrest Head**
Start in Windermere village with the short climb up Orrest Head, about 20 minutes from the station. It was Alfred Wainwright's first view of the fells, and it is still one of the best: the whole lake laid out below you with the Langdale Pikes beyond.

**Lunch: Bowness on Windermere**
Head down to Bowness for lunch by the water, then step on board a Windermere Lake Cruise. The boats run between Bowness, Ambleside and Lakeside, so you can see the lake from the middle of it.

**Afternoon: Ambleside**
Get off at Waterhead for Ambleside: independent shops, galleries, the tiny Bridge House over Stock Beck, and a short walk to Stock Ghyll Force waterfall.

**Evening: check in at Low Wood Bay**
Low Wood Bay Resort & Spa sits right on the shore of Windermere, so the evening is yours to enjoy: time in the spa, then dinner with views across the lake to the fells.

### Day 2: before you head home

**Morning: Grasmere**
After breakfast, drive 15 minutes north to Grasmere. Visit Dove Cottage, William Wordsworth's home, pick up some Grasmere Gingerbread, and walk the path round Grasmere lake if you have time.

## 2 nights in the Lake District

Two nights to see both sides of the Lakes: Windermere and Grasmere in the south, then Keswick and Derwentwater in the north.

### Day 1: Windermere

**Morning: Orrest Head**
Begin with the short walk up Orrest Head from Windermere village for the view Wainwright fell in love with.

**Afternoon: on the water**
Take a Windermere Lake Cruise from Bowness to Ambleside, explore the shops and cafes, and walk out to Stock Ghyll Force.

**Evening: Low Wood Bay**
Check in at Low Wood Bay Resort & Spa on the shore of Windermere. Unwind in the spa, then settle in for dinner overlooking the lake.

### Day 2: Grasmere, Beatrix Potter and the north

**Morning: Grasmere**
Head to Grasmere for Dove Cottage and the Wordsworth Museum, and a stop for Grasmere Gingerbread.

**Lunch and afternoon: Keswick**
Drive north over Dunmail Raise to Keswick, a market town ringed by fells. Walk up to Castlerigg Stone Circle for one of the most dramatic settings in England, then take the Keswick Launch round Derwentwater.

**Evening: Lake District Castle Inn**
Spend your second night at Lake District Castle Inn Hotel & Spa near Bassenthwaite Lake, with the fells all around. A swim, a slow dinner, and an early night before the hills.

### Day 3: a walk before home

**Morning: Catbells or Whinlatter**
For a classic Lakeland walk, climb Catbells above Derwentwater: a short, steep path with huge views from the top. For something gentler, explore the trails at Whinlatter Forest, England's only true mountain forest.

**Before you leave**
Stop at Ashness Bridge for the famous view over Derwentwater, then head home.

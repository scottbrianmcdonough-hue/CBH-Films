Crossing into the third dimension 🏔️

I've been experimenting with turning flat photos into 3D.

Two photos, a coffee by a window and a mountain lake. Split each one into layers, move the camera, and the near things slide past the far ones, like looking out of a train window.

That's parallax, and it's what makes a scene feel real.

The mountain is my favourite. It feels like you could walk right into it.

Still honing the craft, but I'm loving seeing how far one photo can go.

Would you have guessed these started as single photos?

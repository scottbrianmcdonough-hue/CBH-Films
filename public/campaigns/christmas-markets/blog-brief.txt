Unwrap the Magic: Christmas Markets near Classic British Hotels

Searching for Christmas markets nearby? The 2026 dates are out, and a stay with Classic British Hotels turns a market day into a proper weekend away. From handmade gifts and mulled wine to a table already set when you get back, here are the markets worth the trip and the hotels closest to them.

1. Chichester’s Christmas Markets

Chichester’s Christmas Markets return to East and North Street in the heart of the city, two festive weekends of stalls selling gifts, decorations and novelties alongside food and drink from local vendors in the run-up to Christmas. After a wander through the stalls, retreat to Avisford Park Hotel to warm up and round off your festive escape.

2. Canterbury Christmas Market

Canterbury Christmas Market returns for 2026, traditionally one of the last of these markets to close. It’s the biggest in Kent, with around a hundred and twenty decorated huts spread across three parts of the city, one of them inside the Cathedral Precincts.

Hythe Imperial Hotel, Golf & Spa (on the seafront, forty minutes away): its Coast Christmas Joiner Party takes the Fridays of 4, 11 and 18 December, starting with a sparkling drink on arrival, dinner in the Coast Restaurant from seven, then the resident DJ until midnight, with a room and breakfast to follow.
Donnington Manor Hotel (near Sevenoaks): 2-Night and 3-Night Magical Christmas Packages, opening with mulled wine in the Spectrum Bar and dinner in the Chartwell Restaurant on Christmas Eve.

3. Guildford Cathedral Christmas Fair

The Guildford Cathedral Christmas Fair runs 20 and 21 November 2026, ten till four, with more than fifty stalls of handcrafted gifts, decorations, spirits and jewellery, all indoors and warm.

Frensham Pond Hotel & Spa (on the water near Farnham): its 2-Night Magical Christmas Break begins with cream tea in the Watermark Restaurant & Bar, mince pies and mulled wine alongside.
Barnett Hill (closer to the cathedral): a boutique retreat in the Surrey Hills.

4. Leeds Christmas Market

Leeds Christmas Market runs from 19 November to 23 December 2026, and has moved to City Square: chalet huts, an independent craft village, a festive light trail and winter games, with the Little Bird artisan market at Bond Court alongside.

Gomersal Park Hotel (between Leeds and Bradford): a leafy spa hideaway; its 1 Night New Year’s Eve Package brings a sparkling drinks reception and canapés, four courses with coffee and petit fours, then a DJ counting down to 2027.
Waterton Park Hotel & Spa (near Wakefield): a lakeside spa hotel, an easy run into the city.

5. Gloucester Quays Christmas Markets

The Gloucester Quays Christmas Markets take over the historic docks across the weekends leading up to Christmas. More than a hundred and twenty makers come from across the south west with handmade gifts, artisan food and small batch spirits, entry is free, and the outdoor ice rink returns to the quayside alongside them.

Make Hatherley Manor Hotel & Spa your base, ten minutes north of the city. Both Christmas In The Country packages carry full spa access on every day of the stay, with mulled wine and a sharing platter on Christmas Eve and a traditional lunch with Christmas cake at teatime on the day itself.

6. Birmingham Christmas Market

Birmingham’s Frankfurt Christmas Market runs from 5 November to 24 December 2026, the largest authentic German market outside Germany and Austria. Wander Victoria Square and New Street for bratwurst, glühwein, pretzels and handcrafted gifts, with the towering Christmas pyramid at its heart.

After a day of festivities, retreat to Hogarths Solihull. Its Twixmas breaks run 27 to 30 December: bed and breakfast with mulled wine on arrival and a midday checkout, or the two night Retreat & Relax with dinner on the first night.

7. Worcester Victorian Christmas Fayre

Step back in time at the Worcester Victorian Christmas Fayre, running 3 to 6 December 2026. Going since 1992, it fills the city centre with almost two hundred stalls, Victorian costumed characters, stilt walkers and street entertainers.

Make the most of the celebrations with a stay at Hogarths Stone Manor in rural Worcestershire, where its Twixmas bed and breakfast and Retreat & Relax packages run through the festive season.

8. Durham Christmas Festival

Durham Christmas Festival returns for three days, 4 to 6 December 2026, with three markets across the city: the Craft and Producers’ Marquee on Palace Green, the Festive Outdoor Market in the Market Place, and Durham Market Hall. Add Carols for All in the Cathedral and the Children’s Lantern Parade, and for many it’s where Christmas properly begins.

Stay out at the Morritt Hotel & Garage Spa, a country house near Barnard Castle, and make the Garage Spa your reward after a cold day at the market. Set in a converted former garage, it has indoor hot and cold tubs, a sauna, steam room and ice fountain to thaw out in. Gilroy’s Restaurant keeps things traditional, with a Morritt Sunday lunch and afternoon tea.

9. Winchester Christmas Markets

Winchester Cathedral Christmas Market runs from 20 November to 22 December 2026, thirty-three days of wooden chalets modelled on the German markets that started the tradition, set within the cathedral’s historic grounds. The cathedral calls it one of the best in Europe, and its Advent and Christmas services are sung by an internationally acclaimed choir.

Check into Oakley Hall Hotel half an hour away, a Georgian manor of 1795 once owned by the Bramstons, close friends of Jane Austen. Its Two and Three Night Residential Christmas Breaks run through the season, and Oakley Hall sees 2026 out in black tie, adults only, with fireworks over the gardens at midnight.

10. Chester Christmas Markets

Chester Christmas Market runs from 18 November to 22 December 2026, around seventy wooden chalets filling Town Hall Square and Northgate Street, with the Roman walls and the medieval Rows a ten minute walk away.

Settle in at Rowton Hall Hotel & Spa, a Georgian manor of 1779 in eight acres of garden just outside the city. Its Christmas Residential 3 Night Package opens with afternoon tea and a candlelit dinner on Christmas Eve, and mince pies and hot mulled wine wait for anyone coming back from midnight mass.

11. Seven Sisters Christmas Markets

The Seven Sisters Christmas Market returns to the visitor centre on 5 and 6 December 2026, with around 27 stalls of gifts, crafts and local treats, carol singing and free entry in one of the South Downs’ most dramatic coastal settings. After a day on the clifftops, unwind nearby at The Alfriston for a cosy, restful stay.

12. Blair Castle Christmas Fair
Blair Castle Christmas Fair fills the grand Scottish Baronial castle at Blair Atholl on 20 to 22 November 2026, ten till four, with stalls of gifts, crafts and Scottish food and drink in one of Perthshire’s most striking settings.
Just fifteen minutes south in Pitlochry, Atholl Palace Hotel is a Victorian palace in the Scottish Baronial style, set above wooded parkland with a spa, pool and Highland views. Its Christmas party nights and Festive Getaway breaks make an easy base for the fair.

13. Keswick Victorian Fayre
Keswick Victorian Fayre takes over the Lakeland town for a single day, Sunday 6 December 2026, with costumed characters, street entertainers and stalls of local crafts and food beneath the northern fells.
Fifteen minutes north at Bassenthwaite, Lake District Castle Inn Hotel & Spa looks out to the lake and Skiddaw, with a fifteen-metre pool, sauna, steam room and hot tub to warm up in after a day in the cold.

14. Piece Hall Christmas Market
Piece Hall Christmas Market sets up across the weekends before Christmas inside Halifax’s magnificent Georgian cloth hall, its colonnaded courtyard filling with wooden chalets, festive food and makers’ stalls. The 2026 dates are still to be confirmed.
Ten minutes north, Holdsworth House Hotel is a Jacobean manor of 1633 in secluded gardens, family-run for more than sixty years, with an award-winning restaurant and afternoon tea for after the market.

The first stalls go up in early November, and the rest follow within a fortnight. Our Christmas, Twixmas and New Year breaks are bookable now.

Walk the market in the crisp air. Come back to a warm, crackling fire and total relaxation. Wake up with nowhere to be and no rush to leave.
